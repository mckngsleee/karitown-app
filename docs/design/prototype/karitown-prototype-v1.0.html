Design principles

Works without signal. Every save lands on the phone first. The header chip always says what is waiting to upload.

Kilos and pesos, exact underneath. Big numerals, the Android number keypad, and a live breakdown so the owner can check the math in front of the seller.

English labels, Tagalog sublabels. Words owners already use: bawas, karga, pasok, labas.

Readable in sun. Ink on a light ground, 2px rules, deep red for actions. Nothing depends on color alone.

Thumb-first. 48px+ targets, one primary action pinned at the bottom, back arrow and a 5-tab bar like other Android apps.

Light on data and storage. No photos or map tiles; the buyer directory is one cached file.
